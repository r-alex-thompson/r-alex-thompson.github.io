Scenario, baseline and uncertainty

Forecast period: 2026 combines observed January–September with forecast October–December; 2027 combines forecast January–June with assumed-normal July–December. Annual departures compare these paired forecast and reference scenarios. They are not a forecast of every month or a causal no-El-Niño comparison.

Weather spread: 16th–84th percentiles describe 32 equally weighted CFSv2 members. They are not calibrated probabilities. One weather model supplies all members. The date-specific scenario is saved, not a live feed.

NPP: estimates are relative to expected NPP at the forecast date under reference climate, retaining the same baseline trend. NPP bands combine model bootstrap and weather variation. They omit residual and structural uncertainty; anomaly forecast skill remains limited. NPP is plant carbon production, not biomass accumulation, durable sequestration or credit delivery.

Sensitivity: fitted historical associations over 1981–2018, with pooled biome × geography climate relationships and cell trends. Slider departures use each cell’s historical mean; the map uses its paired forecast-reference annual difference. These are different reference definitions. The ±20% rainfall and ±2°C sensitivity grid may include locally unsupported combinations.

Basemap: bundled Natural Earth, no API key. Model inputs: GLASS/MuSyQ NPP, ERA5, CFSv2. Project boundaries: Karnik et al. Historical project status has not been reverified.
