Case Studies

The Delivery Risk Framework in practice.

We apply the same core process across project types. Some questions require a focused technical review. Others require an independent reconstruction of the full carbon forecast.

Evidence → Assumptions → Risk → Delivery
CASE 01
Independent Delivery Forecast
Paragominas, Pará, Brazil

Forest restoration in Paragominas

The questionWhat is this project likely to deliver, and which assumptions create the greatest downside?
Decompose→Test→Stress→Model→Forecast

We independently reconstructed a 5,000-ha restoration forecast, testing baseline dynamics, biomass recovery, leakage, uncertainty, and fire exposure to understand expected credit delivery over time.

Key finding

Near-term delivery was constrained primarily by leakage, despite substantial biological carbon accumulation.

CASE 02
Delivery Screen / Stress Test
Central United States

Agricultural soil carbon

The questionAre the scientific assumptions behind projected soil-carbon delivery defensible?
Decompose→Test→Stress

We tested a commercially plausible SOC forecast against regional evidence and evaluated whether the monitoring design could reliably detect the expected carbon response.

Key finding

The technical question was not simply whether sequestration was plausible, but whether the expected effect was large enough and measurable enough to support credit delivery.
