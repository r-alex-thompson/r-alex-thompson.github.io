04 · Communicate

03 · ABOUT

Forest science, statistics, and carbon-market diligence.

RAT

R. Alex Thompson, PhD
Independent forest carbon consultant
Based in North Carolina · Working globally

I’m a forest ecologist and applied statistician. Most recently, I worked on the investment side at Rubicon Carbon, evaluating nature-based carbon projects and the models used to forecast credit delivery.

My scientific background is in plant physiological ecology: how trees acquire, allocate, store, and lose carbon under changing environmental conditions. I bring that biological perspective into carbon-project modeling, where seemingly small assumptions about growth, mortality, survival, and biomass can materially change projected issuances.

Today I work independently with developers and capital providers that need additional technical capacity, a model built from scratch, or an outside view on whether the numbers hold up.
