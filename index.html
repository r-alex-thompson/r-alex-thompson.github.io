About

Forest science with an investment-side view.

DENDRO ANALYTICS

R. Alex Thompson, PhD
North Carolina · Working globally

Dendro Analytics is led by R. Alex Thompson, PhD, a forest ecologist and applied statistician who most recently worked on the investment side at Rubicon Carbon, evaluating nature-based projects and the models used to forecast credit delivery.

Alex’s scientific background is in plant physiological ecology: how trees acquire, allocate, store, and lose carbon under changing environmental conditions. That biological perspective is applied directly to carbon-project modeling, where assumptions about growth, survival, mortality, and biomass can materially change projected issuances.
