How We Work

Evidence → Assumptions → Risk → Delivery

Every carbon forecast is built on assumptions. We test the evidence behind those assumptions, identify where they create material risk, and translate that risk into expected carbon delivery.

How much carbon is this project likely to deliver, when, and what could cause it to miss?
The Delivery Risk Framework

Follow the forecast back to what drives it.

A repeatable diligence process for moving from the claims behind a carbon forecast to the risks that actually matter for delivery.

01

Decompose

What has to be true?

Identify the ecological, operational, and accounting assumptions driving projected delivery.

02

Test

Does the evidence support it?

Evaluate the data and scientific evidence behind the assumptions that matter.

03

Stress

What could change the outcome?

Test plausible alternatives and identify which uncertainties materially affect delivery.

04

Model

How do the risks interact?

Where needed, independently reconstruct the material components of the forecast and propagate uncertainty.

05

Forecast

What is likely to deliver?

Translate the analysis into expected delivery, downside, and the key drivers of underperformance.
