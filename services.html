Services

Technical support built around the diligence process.

We help nature-based carbon projects move from ecological assumptions to models that can withstand scrutiny. Our work is tailored to the decisions developers need to make and the risks investors and buyers need to understand.

Developers

Build models that are ready for capital.

We support projects from early concept through diligence, helping teams create defensible ex-ante models, strengthen ecological assumptions, and reduce friction when investors, buyers, registries, or third parties review the project.

01

Full ex-ante carbon modeling

End-to-end project models covering baseline, growth, survival, mortality, uncertainty, leakage, and other methodology-eligible carbon pools.

02

Growth & survival modeling

Species-, site-, and ecosystem-specific trajectories for projects that already have other model components in place.

03

Assumption development

Empirical literature review, allometry, biomass accumulation, mortality, establishment, and parameter selection with transparent supporting evidence.

04

Uncertainty & scenario analysis

High, base, and low cases plus explicit treatment of parameter uncertainty and model sensitivity.

05

Climate & delivery-risk analysis

Evaluate how changing climate conditions may affect growth, survival, mortality, and projected issuances so project teams can identify risk before diligence exposes it.

Investors & Buyers

Understand what is really driving delivery risk.

We independently pressure-test project models so diligence teams can distinguish reasonable ecological uncertainty from assumptions that materially change expected credit delivery.

01

Independent model review

Review ex-ante carbon models for ecological plausibility, internal consistency, methodology alignment, and sensitivity to key assumptions.

02

Rebuild & benchmark

Develop an independent carbon trajectory to compare against developer projections and identify where the models diverge.

03

Growth, survival & mortality diligence

Assess whether biological assumptions are supported by site conditions, species ecology, empirical evidence, and management plans.

04

Uncertainty & downside analysis

Quantify how uncertainty and adverse ecological scenarios flow through to expected issuances and delivery timing.

05

Technical diligence support

Translate complex ecological and modeling issues into concise diligence findings, decision-relevant questions, and clear risk framing for investment committees, buyers, and transaction teams.

Typical deliverables

  • Transparent Excel carbon model
  • Technical modeling memo with references
  • Key assumptions and uncertainties memo
  • High / base / low scenario outputs
  • Climate or sensitivity analysis where relevant

How engagements work

Projects can engage us for a full model, a focused technical workstream, or an independent review. Scope is built around the stage of the project and the specific diligence questions that need to be answered.

That means you do not need to buy a full-suite engagement when the real issue is one assumption, one carbon pool, or one piece of the model.

Make the technical work easier to defend.

Whether you are preparing a project for funding or deciding whether a carbon forecast is credible, we can help clarify the ecology behind the model.
